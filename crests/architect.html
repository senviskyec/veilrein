Position

As the Architect, Wwyrim oversees the physical development of Veilrein and serves as the Council's primary authority on construction, infrastructure, and urban planning.

The Architect shapes the physical city of Veilrein. Deciding how it grows, districts are built, how infrastructure connects, and what standards new construction must follow.

Political Role

Cyn is one of the more mysterious members of Veilrein's Council. They were elected for the position after the previous Architect disapeared under mysterious circumstances. Cyn has been known to be a very private individual, and little is known about their personal life or political affiliations. What is known is that they very much enjoy their work, that despite the role of education falling on the shoulder of The Witch, Cyn is the one who built Veilrein's University.

It is on record that Cyn had no desire to be a part of the Council, and that they were only convinced to join by the previous Architect, who was a close friend of theirs. He has a strong passion for sustainability and has been known to be a strong advocate for green energy and eco-friendly building practices. He is primraly known for his push for advancing Create and Computer technology, being the pioneer in the creation of modern airships and the first to implement a city-wide power grid.

One of the first things Cyn did after joining the Council was doing a complete overhaul of the cities districts. Creating massive airships to move entire buildings to new locations, and creating dedicated Industrial District which the City was lacking for years. His latest project was the Hex Gates. Giant portals that allowed for instant travel between other cities and even other worlds. The Hex Gates are still in development, but they have already been used to connect Veilrein to the city of Elysium, and the world of Aetheria.
